10. METRIC DEFINITIONS
==================================================================================================================================
MAE             -> Average absolute dollar error.
Median_AE       -> Typical dollar error; 50% of predictions have smaller absolute error.
MSE             -> Mean squared error; strongly penalizes large dollar mistakes.
RMSE            -> Dollar error metric that penalizes large mistakes more heavily than MAE.
R2              -> Proportion of price variance explained by the model. Closer to 1 is better.
MAPE            -> Average absolute percentage difference between prediction and actual price.
Median_APE      -> Typical absolute percentage error.
sMAPE           -> Symmetric percentage error based on both actual and predicted price.
WAPE            -> Total absolute dollar error divided by total actual dollar value.
Bias            -> Mean actual minus predicted price. Near zero means little systematic bias.
Median_Bias     -> Median signed prediction error.
NRMSE_Mean      -> RMSE relative to the average actual price.
P75_APE         -> 75% of predictions have percentage error below this value.
P90_APE         -> 90% of predictions have percentage error below this value.
P95_APE         -> 95% of predictions have percentage error below this value.
P99_APE         -> 99% of predictions have percentage error below this value.
Within_5        -> Percentage of predictions within ±5% of actual price.
Within_10       -> Percentage of predictions within ±10% of actual price.
Within_15       -> Percentage of predictions within ±15% of actual price.
Within_20       -> Percentage of predictions within ±20% of actual price.
Over_20         -> Percentage of predictions with more than 20% error.
Over_30         -> Percentage of predictions with more than 30% error.
Over_50         -> Percentage of predictions with more than 50% error.
