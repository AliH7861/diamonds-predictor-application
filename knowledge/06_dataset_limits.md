# Dataset Coverage and Limits

## Available fields

The dataset contains price, carat, cut, color, clarity, depth, table, and x, y, z dimensions. Engineered geometry and value features are calculated from those columns.

## Unavailable fields

The dataset has no customer ID, customer demographics, certification laboratory, certificate number, country or mine of origin, named diamond shape, fluorescence, symmetry, polish, inclusion type, seller, sales channel, transaction date, or live market price.

## Safe conclusions

The project can compare supplied rows, summarize historical relationships, estimate price or clarity with saved models, and infer broad purchase-profile segments. It cannot verify a physical stone, identify a buyer, prove origin, or guarantee a current selling price.
