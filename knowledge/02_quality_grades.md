# Diamond Quality Grades

## Cut

Cut affects brightness, sparkle, and visual performance. The dataset contains Fair, Good, Very Good, Premium, and Ideal cut grades. Cut is different from shape: the dataset does not provide named shapes such as round, oval, or princess.

## Clarity

Clarity describes inclusions and blemishes observed during grading. This project presents five clarity families: I, SI, VS, VVS, and IF. The source rows contain detailed subgrades, but the assistant combines them into those five families. VS diamonds often provide a practical balance because small inclusions may be difficult to see without magnification. VVS and IF represent progressively cleaner and usually more expensive choices.

## Color

The dataset uses color grades D through J. D is the most colorless end of this range and J shows more body color. Differences between neighboring grades can be subtle, and the appearance also depends on lighting and the setting.
