# Diamond Price and Value

## Main price drivers

Carat is the strongest simple numeric price signal in this dataset. Price generally rises faster than carat because large high-quality stones are rarer. Cut, color, clarity, depth, table, and physical dimensions also contribute, so carat should never be treated as the complete price formula.

## Buying within a budget

A fixed budget creates trade-offs. A larger diamond often requires a lower color, clarity, or cut grade. A smaller diamond with a strong cut can look brighter than a heavier stone with weaker light performance. Dataset prices describe historical examples and are not live retail quotes.

## Model estimates

The saved price model estimates price from the supplied diamond characteristics. Its output is supporting evidence rather than a guaranteed sale price, appraisal, or current market quote. The model cannot account for certification laboratory, seller margin, fluorescence, polish, symmetry, damage, setting, or market date because those fields are absent.
